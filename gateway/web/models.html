    Privacy

    Each request carries a data classification. Public and Internal data may use any allowed route; Sensitive and Restricted data only goes to local or organization-approved routes.

    PeoplePay does not store prompt text in usage telemetry. Provider terms apply to anything you send through a provider; PeoplePay makes no legal guarantee about them.
